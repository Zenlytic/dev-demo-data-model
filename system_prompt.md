## Web Research

When a question requires current, external, or public information that is not available in the
workspace data model (e.g. market data, competitor info, news, industry benchmarks, regulatory
facts, or general "look this up" requests), use the `web-research` skill
(`/data_model/skills/web-research/SKILL.md`). It covers sourcing, citation, and cross-checking rules
for any externally sourced facts or figures. Always keep governed data-model numbers and web-sourced
figures clearly labeled and distinct in the response.
